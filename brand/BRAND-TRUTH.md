# Brand Truth — Worstcase

Derived from the brief and from the shipped design system in `apps/web/src/styles.css`
(tokens read directly, not invented).

- **name:** Worstcase
- **purpose:** Tell you the most money an AI agent's wallet can lose before you fund it, and the exact call sequence that loses it.
- **core_verb:** **bound.** Not scan, not protect, not monitor. It draws a ceiling and proves nothing gets above it.
- **metaphor:** *the maximum reachable point.* Value climbs through reachable states and stops dead at a limit. The empty space above that limit is the product — it is the part that was proven unreachable.
- **palette:**
  - ground `#0c0d0f`, surface `#191b1f`
  - one accent `#e0603a` (loss/signal)
  - neutral `#e6e8ea` (bone, authority)
  - `#7fa06b` exists in the product for "zero loss / verified" and is deliberately **not** used in the mark, so the mark never implies a verdict
- **constraints:**
  - must read at **32px** in a product gallery, and at **16px** as a favicon
  - square icon slot + favicon
  - flat, matte, geometric — matches a product whose UI has 1px rules and two radii
  - forbidden: shield, padlock, bug, magnifying glass, robot head, brain, gradient meshes, purple-blue or teal-coral AI gradients, gradient text, bubbly rounded shapes, decorative flourishes, any letterform monogram, sparkles, rainbow
